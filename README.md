# Trade Under Pressure: India's Push for Export-Led Growth Amid US Tariffs and Rising Import Costs (2025–26)

A school Economics project (CBSE Class XII, Lotus Valley International School, Gurugram) applying Class 11–12 macroeconomics to India's trade situation in 2025–26.

## Question
How do core concepts such as tariffs, balance of payments, exchange rates, elasticity and cost-push inflation explain what happened to India's economy when US tariffs and higher oil prices hit at the same time?

## Key themes
- US tariffs on Indian exports rose as high as 50% in 2025, then eased after a February 2026 trade deal.
- India's response combined export diversification and crude oil supplier diversification.
- Inelastic demand for fuel passed higher oil prices into domestic inflation (CPI 4.38% in June 2026).
- Growth held up despite the shocks, with the RBI revising its FY 2026–27 forecast from 6.9% to 6.6%.

## Sources and limitations
Based on secondary data from the RBI, the Press Information Bureau, UN DESA and news and trade-data sources. Events were still unfolding in July 2026, so figures may be revised.
